## Local archived payloads

Historical payloads live under `.local/archive/` and are intentionally ignored
by Git. The dated machine-readable archive manifest records original path,
purpose, size, digest, destination, and verification. `.local/dracula.sqlite3`
and recorded human games are protected operational state, not archive payloads.
