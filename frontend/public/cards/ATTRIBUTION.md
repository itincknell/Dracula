# Card assets

The 54 PNG files are copied from Kenney Playing Cards Pack 1.0. Kenney released
the pack under CC0. The project-local `Cards (large)` directory is the immutable
source; `npm run prepare:cards` maps its 52 suited cards and two Jokers to the
canonical Dracula card IDs. `V1` and `V2` are the Vampire cards.

Source: https://www.kenney.nl/assets/playing-cards-pack
